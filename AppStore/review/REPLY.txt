2. Purpose and audience

Zero Fret is a guitar and bass tuner. A needle asks you to look at the phone while both hands are on the pegs. This draws a string instead. It wobbles at the beat rate against the target and goes still when the note is in. One haptic tick on arrival. Optional "Feel the beat" taps once per beat in the hand, so you can tune without looking — including if you cannot see the screen. Audience: guitarists and bassists, beginners included.

3. How to reach the main features

No login, no sample files, no demo account.

Open the app. Allow the microphone. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted, and the mic stops when the app leaves the foreground. There is no background-audio mode. Pluck an open string. Tuning (top left) switches among Standard, Drop D, E♭, D Standard, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, 7-string, 8-string, and bass 4/5/6. Settings (top right): reference pitch (default A=440), tolerance, haptic tick, Feel the beat, response speed. Tap a string chip to pin; tap again to release. Landscape widens the string. Reduce Motion holds it still and keeps the cents.

4. External services

None. No network. No data provider, authentication service, payment processor, analytics, or AI service. Detection, display, and haptics run on device (AVFoundation, Accelerate, SwiftUI, UIKit). Privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false.

5. Regional differences

None. Same features in every region. No region-gated content. Interface is English. Targets are equal-temperament MIDI notes derived from the player's reference A, not from the storefront.

6. Regulated industry / protected material

Not applicable. Not a medical, financial, or licensed-content service. No recordings, scores, or other protected third-party material. Tuning presets are standard open-string note names.

Zero Fret is a guitar and bass tuner that shows you a vibrating string instead of a needle. Pluck a string and watch its wobble slow and stop as the note comes into tune. Turn on Feel the Beat to tune by touch without looking at the screen.

• Guitar and bass tuning
• Chromatic fallback
• Haptic beat feedback
• Adjustable reference pitch
• No accounts, ads, analytics, or subscriptions
• No network connection
• Audio is processed live on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted

The microphone runs only while Zero Fret is open.
